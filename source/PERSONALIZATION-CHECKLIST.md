# Make the notebook yours

No extra stock photographs are needed. Your actual drawings would be particularly valuable: send 3–6 full-resolution scans or evenly lit photographs, with a title, medium (pencil, ink, watercolor, etc.), year, and one sentence about each. A flat, uncropped scan at 2000 pixels or more is ideal. The best additions now are your words and context.

1. **Photo captions:** send each filename with location, approximate date, and one sentence about why the moment matters. For photos with other people, tell me the names only if you want them shown. Identify the gate location and the visit behind the guest badge; no story has been invented for either.
2. **Your introduction:** 100–200 words in your natural voice: what you study, what you care about, what you hope to build, and one detail outside work. Correct any current bio or project facts.
3. **Your writing:** one 400–800 word article or a voice-note transcript on a real experiment, a physics idea you love, or a lesson from a project. Existing original essays need your review to reflect your own opinions.
4. **Your reading:** your five favorite books, ratings if you use them, and 2–3 sentences on what changed your thinking in each. The current summaries explain the books; your reflections will make the section personal.
5. **Projects:** name, what it does, your role, current stage, one screenshot, and a real public link for each project. Current project links lead to Contact until you supply destinations.
6. **Contact and social links:** the email you want publicly shown and exact social/profile URLs. Contact messages are stored in the Hostinger dashboard; automatic email notifications require an email delivery service.
7. **Current life:** what you are reading, learning, and working on this month. These make useful homepage updates.

## Optional future photos

If you want more, send 3–5 genuine photos of a book with your notes, an engineering experiment, your workspace, or an interesting place. Original full-resolution JPG/PNG/HEIC files are best. Include a landscape option (at least 1600 px wide) for featured stories and portrait options for the gallery. Natural lighting is welcome; there is no need for a matching filter.

## Publishing

Upload the contents of the Hostinger ZIP to `public_html`, then follow HOSTINGER-SETUP.md. Configure the database and private configuration file before opening the admin setup page. The ChatGPT website is a static design preview; real admin edits, messages, and subscriptions run on the PHP Hostinger version.
